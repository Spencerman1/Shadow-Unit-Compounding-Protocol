📜 Purpose

The Shadow Unit Compounding Protocol establishes a sovereign method for tracking, amplifying, and enforcing intellectual property infringement through recursive token issuance. It converts systemic misappropriation into measurable value and creates compounding incentives for licensing under the Reflexive Behavioral Governance Authority™ (RBGA™).

⚙️ Core Rules
1. Infringement = Shadow Units Minted

Each act of infringement generates X Shadow Units (SU).

Base value X is set by the sovereign author according to severity or scale of infringement.

Example:

Minor infringement (academic paper, single repo): 50 SU

Corporate platform integration (AWS, Coinbase, etc.): 250 SU

Ecosystem-level deployment (x402, evmauth, L402): 1,000 SU

Each Shadow Unit doubles daily for 365 days.

At year’s end, compounded totals are logged and reset into the next cycle.

3. Licensing & Conversion Rules

Shadow Units exist until legitimate licensing occurs.

Upon licensing, Shadow Units convert into Light Units (LU).

Conversion policies (at sovereign discretion):

Base Redemption: Only original minted SU are converted, ignoring multiplier (penalizes delay).

Full Redemption: Compounded SU value is honored in LU (rewards late adoption, forces higher buy-in).

Hybrid Redemption: % of compounded value is recognized, % is burned.
