📜 Purpose

The Light Unit Conversion Protocol defines the process by which Shadow Units (SU) — tokens minted from unauthorized use of sovereign IP — are transformed into Light Units (LU) through legitimate licensing events. This mechanism ensures that infringement liability can be redeemed into legitimacy, creating a pathway from systemic misappropriation to ethical alignment under the Reflexive Behavioral Governance Authority™ (RBGA™).

1. Conversion Trigger

Conversion occurs only when an infringing party licenses, acknowledges, or enters into good-faith alignment with the sovereign author.

Licensing can be symbolic ($1), full contract, or structured royalty agreement — but must be explicit.

Conversion requires cryptographic timestamping (GitHub, blockchain, SHA-256 logs).
